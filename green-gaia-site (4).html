The Mythos

Green Gaia is the earth given voice — a guardian born of moss, ivy, and ancestral light, standing between the world and what threatens to unmake it. At the center of her story is Greta Green, and the shadow she must face is Ozzmera.

Greta Green

The protagonist — a young woman who discovers she carries Gaia's spirit within her, and must learn to wield it before the world runs out of time.

Ozzmera

The force that opposes her — a corrupting power that feeds on what the earth loses when it's forgotten, poisoned, or paved over.
